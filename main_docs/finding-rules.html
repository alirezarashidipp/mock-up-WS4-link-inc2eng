Prepare the supplier name.

StepIncident nameEngagement field
What does the cleaner change?

Lowercase both sides. Delete periods, expand & to and, and turn separators into spaces. Remove whole stop words and legal terms such as and, solutions and ltd. Digits and apostrophes stay.

Apply one whole-name alias on the incident side. Remove spaces for the comparison; keep the original values in the CSV.

Which input rows and fields are searched?

CLASSIFIED_INCIDENTS.csv · Select the exact class SUPPLIER; skip a blank name or the whole value Unknown, ignoring case and surrounding spaces.

ENG_ALL.csv · Read the engagement ID and each available nonempty field in configured order. Examples here use THIRD_PARTY_NAME, then THIRD_PARTY_NORMALISED_NAME.

How is similarity calculated?

Count the fewest insertions, deletions and substitutions needed. Similarity = 1 − (edits ÷ longer name length). Cisco → misco gives 1 − 1/5 = 80%.
