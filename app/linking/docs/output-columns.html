Linking output columns

Linking appends these eight columns to each assessed CSV row.

ColumnMeaning
incident_evidence_citation Excerpts from the incident relevant to this candidate.
engagement_evidence_citation Excerpts from this engagement that support or challenge the link.
incident_evidence_citation_strength How clearly the incident identifies the supplier service involved. All its rows use the first batch’s rating.
engagement_evidence_citation_strength How clearly the engagement describes the service it covers.
link_rationale The evidence or gap that explains the decision.
link_decision Whether this engagement covers the service implicated in the incident.
link_decision_confidence_reason Why the model assigned this confidence level.
link_decision_confidence Confidence in the decision (LOW, MEDIUM or HIGH).
